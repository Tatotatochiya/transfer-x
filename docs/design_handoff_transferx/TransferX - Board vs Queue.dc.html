What to ask a real sporting director

Show them both and ask one question: when you open this on a Tuesday morning, what are you trying to find out? If the answer is "whether anything has moved", the board wins and the light redesign is all you need. If it is "what I have to reply to", the queue wins.

A hybrid is also legitimate: the queue at the top, the board kept below it. It hedges the bet, at the cost of a longer page.
