# Product Decisions

## Purpose

A record of significant product decisions — what was decided, the alternatives considered, and why. This exists so the reasoning behind a decision survives longer than the conversation it was made in, and so a later contributor (human or AI) doesn't accidentally re-litigate or reverse a decision without knowing why it was made.

## Scope

In scope: product-level decisions (what to build, what not to build, scope trade-offs). For system-design decisions, see [`../../architecture/decisions/README.md`](../../architecture/decisions/README.md) instead.
